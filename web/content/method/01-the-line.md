## The line

The line is the number of job postings on Indeed on 1 February 2020, before the pandemic and the hiring freeze that followed. Indeed Hiring Lab sets that day to 100 for every country and sector. A reading of 66 means postings are 34% below the line; 112 means 12% above.

Each reading is a seven-day trailing average, seasonally adjusted by Indeed. Thawline turns the daily readings into weekly averages for weeks ending on Friday. Statements and the home page place a sector by its latest weekly reading, and judge its direction from the average of the last four weeks, which moves less from week to week. The Thaw Index uses that four-week average too. A sector within {{line.at_line_band}} points of 100 is described as at the line.
