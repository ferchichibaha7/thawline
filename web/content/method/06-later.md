## Methods for sections not yet published

These rules are fixed now so they can be checked before any result appears.

### Aftermath verdicts (version av-1)

Evidence tiers: A is the company's own statement, B is reporting by an allow-listed publisher, C is a public artifact anyone can check, such as a job board or a legally required layoff notice, and D is an unverified individual report. Tier D never affects a verdict.

Verdicts are checked in order: Walked back needs one tier A reversal, or two tier B reports of rehiring from different publishers. Mixed needs one tier B or C reversal, or two tier B reports of quality problems. Too early applies within 180 days of the cut. Holding needs 180 days, no reversal and at least one positive result. Everything else is Unclear. A moderator approves every verdict change, and every change is logged publicly.

### Pressure gauge (version pg-1)

Nine indicators across chips, cloud, applications, adoption and markets, each ranked against its own history so that 100 is maximum strain. The gauge is their weighted mean: 0 to 33 Calm, 34 to 66 Stretched, 67 to 100 Strained. It measures pressure. It does not give a date or a probability of a crash.
