## Sources and their limits

| Source | What Thawline uses | License | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indeed Hiring Lab Job Postings Index | Daily postings index by country and by occupational sector, from the public repository `hiring-lab/job_postings_tracker` | CC BY 4.0, cite Indeed Hiring Lab | Postings measure demand, not hires. Sectors are Indeed's own groups of job titles. Indeed publishes no sector data for Ireland, the Netherlands, Spain or Italy. Indeed revises history when it updates its seasonal adjustment. |
| US Bureau of Labor Statistics | Employment in computer systems design and in software publishers; job openings in the information sector | Public domain | United States only. Monthly, with revisions in the following two months. |
| FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis | 10-year Treasury yield and the US unemployment rate | Public domain, citation requested | Used for context and, later, for the pressure gauge. |

FRED also republishes Indeed's series, but marks them as copyrighted with pre-approval required, so Thawline takes Indeed data only from Indeed's own repository. FRED marks the Nasdaq Composite the same way, so it is not used.
