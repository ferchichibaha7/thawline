## Known gaps

- Role scores use sector data. Roles in the same sector share a score until company job-board data adds breadth, entry access and competition.
- Design roles use Indeed's Arts & Entertainment sector, whose examples are designers and artists, so the score reflects designers broadly, not only product designers.
- Indeed publishes sector data for six countries: the United States, the United Kingdom, France, Germany, Canada and Australia.
- The pressure gauge needs a stock market series that can be republished. FRED's Nasdaq Composite cannot be, so that indicator waits for another source.
