# Code of conduct

Thawline is about people who lost work or fear losing it. Treat everyone here with that in mind.

- Be respectful and specific. Criticise work, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or private channels.
- Do not post personal information about anyone, including people who work at companies Thawline tracks.
- Do not share confidential or leaked material.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and block people who break these rules. Report problems to the contact address on the About page. Reports are handled privately.
